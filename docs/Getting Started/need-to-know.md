## Sourcing and lot tracking

Every tea in the teaCapital collection is sourced from a fictional network of Japanese producers and assigned a unique lot number on arrival. The lot ties the tea to its origin, harvest date, and tasting notes so you can trace exactly what you are drinking. If you reorder a tea you enjoyed, the lot number helps the team locate the same batch — or recommend the closest match if that lot has sold out.

Lot information is printed on the pouch label and recorded in your order history. This level of traceability is part of teaCapital's fictional commitment to transparency: you should always know where your tea came from and how it was handled before it reached your cup.

## Caffeine and dietary information

Japanese green teas contain moderate levels of caffeine, typically between twenty and thirty milligrams per cup when brewed at the recommended temperature and time. Powdered tea delivers more caffeine per serving because you consume the entire ground leaf rather than discarding it after steeping. If you are sensitive to caffeine, consider brewing with cooler water or shortening the steep time, both of which reduce extraction.

All teaCapital teas are single-ingredient products — nothing is added during processing or packaging. The teas are handled in a facility that does not process nuts, dairy, or gluten-containing grains, making them suitable for most dietary restrictions. Flavor notes listed in the [tea collection](/docs/tea-collection) describe naturally occurring aromas and tastes, not added ingredients.

## Shipping and returns

Orders ship within two business days of payment confirmation, and standard delivery takes three to seven business days depending on your location. Expedited shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee. During peak seasons — particularly the spring first-flush harvest — processing times may extend by one to two days due to higher order volume.

Unopened, undamaged pouches may be returned within thirty days of delivery for a full refund. Opened tea cannot be returned for food-safety reasons, but if you are unsatisfied with the quality of an opened pouch, contact the teaCapital support team with your lot number and a brief description of the issue. The team will work with you on a replacement or credit on a case-by-case basis.
