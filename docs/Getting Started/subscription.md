## At a glance

| Detail | Description |

| Delivery frequency | Every two weeks, monthly, or every two months |

| Cancellation | Cancel anytime from your account — remaining cycle ships as scheduled |

## Choosing a plan

teaCapital offers three subscription tiers designed around how much tea you drink and how adventurous you want to be. The Explorer plan ships a single tea each cycle and is a good starting point if you are new to Japanese tea or prefer to focus on one style at a time. The Connoisseur plan includes three teas — typically one from each style category — so you can compare steamed green, roasted, and powdered teas side by side. The Curator plan ships five teas and often features limited-run lots or seasonal harvests that are not available in the regular collection.

Each plan lets you set a delivery cadence of every two weeks, monthly, or every two months. If you drink tea daily, a two-week cadence keeps your supply fresh without overstocking. Occasional drinkers usually find the monthly or bi-monthly cadence more practical, since unopened pouches stay fresh longer when stored properly.

## Managing your subscription

You can pause, skip, or swap teas from the subscription dashboard in your teaCapital account. Pausing freezes your next shipment and billing cycle for up to sixty days without canceling the plan. Skipping advances the cycle by one period — useful if you still have tea on hand and want to wait for the next rotation. Swapping lets you replace a scheduled tea with another selection from the current [tea collection](/docs/tea-collection) as long as the request is made at least five days before the ship date.

Billing is processed at the start of each cycle. If you upgrade or downgrade mid-cycle, the difference is prorated so you only pay for what ships. Receipts and upcoming charge dates are visible on the billing tab of your account, and you can update your payment method or shipping address at any time without affecting the current cycle.

## Gifting a subscription

Prepaid gift subscriptions are available in three-, six-, and twelve-cycle options. The recipient receives an email with a redemption link and can choose their own plan tier and delivery cadence. Gift cycles begin when the recipient activates the link, not when the purchase is made, so there is no rush to redeem. If the recipient already has an active subscription, the gift cycles are added to the end of their current plan.

Once all prepaid cycles have shipped, the recipient can choose to continue with a regular subscription or let the plan expire. No payment information is collected from the recipient unless they opt to continue.
