## How subscriptions work

Each teaCapital subscription delivers a curated selection of Japanese teas to your door on a recurring schedule. When you sign up, you choose a plan based on how often you brew and how many styles you want to explore. The Explorer plan is designed for people who are new to Japanese tea and want a low-commitment way to taste steamed green, roasted, and powdered styles over several months. The Enthusiast and Connoisseur plans increase both the frequency and the variety, giving regular drinkers a steady rotation without the need to reorder manually.

Every shipment includes a brewing card for each tea with the recommended water temperature, steep time, and leaf-to-water ratio. These baselines match the recipes in the [brewing guide](/docs/brewing-guide), so you can cross-reference and adjust as you learn your preferences. If a particular tea stands out, you can add it to your next order as a one-time extra without changing your plan.

## Managing your subscription

You can pause, skip, or cancel your subscription at any time from your fictional teaCapital account dashboard. Pausing freezes your plan for up to three billing cycles — your preferences and pricing are preserved, and deliveries resume automatically when the pause period ends. Skipping lets you hold a single shipment while keeping the regular schedule intact for the following cycle.

Plan changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. If you upgrade mid-cycle, the price difference is prorated and applied to your next invoice. Downgrades also take effect at the next cycle, and any unused credit rolls forward. teaCapital does not charge cancellation fees; once cancelled, your account retains your tasting history and preferences in case you decide to resubscribe later.
