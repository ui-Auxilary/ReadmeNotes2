## Order and account issues

These are the most frequently reported problems with teaCapital orders and accounts.

### My order has not arrived

If your order has not arrived within the expected delivery window:

1. Check the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email.
2. Verify that the delivery address on your account matches the address you intended.
3. Contact teaCapital support with your order number if the tracking status has not updated in 48 hours.

Delivery windows vary by region:

| Region | Standard delivery | Express delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic | 5–7 business days | 2–3 business days |
| International | 10–21 business days | 5–7 business days |

### I cannot log in to my account

If you are unable to access your teaCapital account:

- **Forgotten password:** Use the "Reset password" link on the login page. The reset email arrives within five minutes.
- **Locked account:** After five failed login attempts, your account locks for 30 minutes. Wait and try again, or contact support to unlock it immediately.
- **Email not recognized:** Confirm you are using the same email address you registered with. Check for typos or try alternate email addresses you may have used.

### I was charged twice

Duplicate charges usually appear as a pending authorization that drops off within 3–5 business days. If both charges post to your statement, contact support with your order number and a screenshot of the charges.

## Brewing and equipment issues

For tea-specific brewing adjustments (water temperature, steep time, leaf-to-water ratio), see the [brewing guide](/docs/brewing-guide). The issues below cover equipment and environment problems.

### My kettle does not reach the target temperature

Electric kettles with a temperature dial can drift over time. To verify accuracy:

1. Bring water to a full boil and confirm the display reads 100°C / 212°F.
2. If the reading is off by more than 3°C, recalibrate the kettle according to the manufacturer's instructions or use a separate thermometer.
3. Altitude affects boiling point — at elevations above 1,500 m, water boils below 100°C. Adjust your target temperature accordingly.

### My teapot retains flavors from previous brews

Unglazed clay and ceramic teapots absorb oils from tea over time. To minimize flavor carryover:

- Rinse the pot with hot water before and after each use.
- Avoid using soap, which can leave residue in porous materials.
- Dedicate one teapot to a single tea style if cross-contamination is noticeable.

If the flavor persists, soak the pot in plain hot water for one hour, then air-dry it completely before the next use.

## When to contact support

If the steps above do not resolve your issue, reach out to teaCapital support with the following details:

- Your account email address
- The order number (if applicable)
- A brief description of the problem and the steps you have already tried
