## Brewing problems

Most brewing issues trace back to one of three variables: water temperature, leaf-to-water ratio, or steep time. If your cup tastes bitter or astringent, the water was likely too hot or the leaves sat in it too long. Lower the temperature by five to ten degrees Celsius and shorten the steep by fifteen seconds, then taste again. Small adjustments make a noticeable difference with Japanese green teas because their delicate compounds extract quickly.

A weak or watery cup usually means the opposite — not enough leaf or too much water. Increase the leaf amount by one gram while keeping the water volume the same, or reduce the water by twenty to thirty milliliters. If you are cold-brewing, extend the refrigeration time by an hour or two rather than adding more leaf, since cold extraction is slower and benefits from patience.

## Storage and freshness

Tea that smells flat or papery has likely been exposed to air, moisture, or strong odors. Transfer opened tea into an opaque, airtight container and store it away from spices, coffee, and direct sunlight. Refrigeration can extend the life of opened tea, but always let the container reach room temperature before opening it so condensation does not form on the leaves.

If you suspect your tea has passed its prime, brew a small test cup before discarding the rest. Stale tea is generally safe to drink — it simply lacks the vibrant flavor and aroma of a fresh batch. When the dry leaves have shifted from bright green to dull yellow-brown, or the brewed cup tastes noticeably flat, it is time to replace the pouch.

## Order and delivery issues

If a shipment has not arrived within the expected window, verify that the address on file is correct and that no payment errors are flagged in your account. Seasonal demand — especially around spring harvest — can extend lead times by several days. Check the order confirmation email for a tracking link, and allow an extra business day before reaching out to support.

For damaged or incorrect items, photograph the package and its contents as soon as you open it. Include the lot number printed on the pouch label when you contact the teaCapital support team, so the issue can be matched to the specific batch and resolved quickly.
