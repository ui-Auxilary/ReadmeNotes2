## At a glance

| Step | What happens | Estimated time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export your data | Download your order history and taste preferences from your previous provider | 5–10 minutes |

## Exporting from your previous provider

Before you switch, gather the data you want to bring with you. Most tea subscription services let you export your order history, saved preferences, and tasting notes as a CSV or PDF file. Download this file and keep it accessible — you will reference it when setting up your teaCapital account. If your previous provider does not offer an export feature, note your favorite teas, preferred flavor profiles, and typical order frequency manually so you have a reference point.

Pay attention to any remaining prepaid cycles or credits on your old account. Cancel or pause your existing subscription before your next billing date to avoid overlapping charges. If you have unused gift credits, check whether they can be redeemed before cancellation or transferred to another recipient.

## Setting up your teaCapital account

Create a new account on the teaCapital website and complete the flavor profile questionnaire during registration. The questionnaire asks about your preferred tea styles — steamed green, roasted, or powdered — as well as flavor preferences such as vegetal, nutty, or umami-forward. Your answers help the system recommend teas from the [tea collection](/docs/tea-collection) that align with your taste.

Once your account is active, navigate to the import section of your dashboard. You can upload the CSV file from your previous provider, and the system will attempt to map your past orders to equivalent teaCapital styles. Review the suggested matches carefully — a "sencha" from one provider may differ in steaming depth or shading from the teaCapital version, so tasting the recommended match before committing to a full subscription cycle is a good practice.

## Mapping your favorites

The mapping step is where most of the migration effort lives. Open the side-by-side comparison view in your dashboard, which displays your imported tea names alongside the closest teaCapital equivalents. Each suggestion includes a style tag, flavor notes, and a link to the relevant brewing baseline in the [brewing guide](/docs/brewing-guide). Accept, reject, or swap each suggestion until your mapped list reflects the teas you actually want to receive.

If a tea from your previous provider has no close equivalent in the current teaCapital collection, the system flags it and offers to notify you when a similar lot becomes available. You can also browse the full collection manually and add any tea that catches your interest, regardless of whether it appeared in your import file.

## Completing the switch

After mapping is complete, choose a [subscription](/docs/subscription) plan and delivery cadence that matches your previous ordering rhythm. The dashboard pre-fills a recommended plan based on your import data — for example, if you previously received three teas per month, it suggests the Connoisseur plan on a monthly cadence. Adjust the recommendation if your needs have changed, then confirm your payment method and shipping address to activate the first cycle.

Your first teaCapital shipment includes a printed quick-start card with brewing baselines for each tea in the box. Use it alongside the [brewing guide](/docs/brewing-guide) to prepare your first cups, and update your flavor profile after tasting so future cycles reflect your evolving preferences.
