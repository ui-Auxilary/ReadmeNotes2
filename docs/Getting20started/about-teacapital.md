## What teaCapital does

teaCapital is a fictional tea company centered on teas sourced from Japan. Its sample collection is designed to help new and experienced drinkers alike choose a tea by flavor, preparation method, and moment of the day.

The brand's fictional approach is all about making origin and preparation easier to understand. Each tea comes with a clear style, a recommended brewing starting point, and plenty of room for you to adjust the cup to your own preference.

## How the collection is organized

Start by choosing the tea experience you're in the mood for:

- Choose **steamed green tea** for fresh, vegetal, or savory flavors.
- Choose **roasted tea** for warm, nutty, or toasty flavors.
- Choose **powdered tea** when you want to whisk the whole leaf into water.

Head over to the [tea collection](/docs/tea-collection) guide to compare these styles. Then use the [brewing guide](/docs/brewing-guide) to prepare your chosen tea.

## Sample sourcing principles

The fictional teaCapital sourcing program follows three simple principles:

1. **Know the tea style.** Record whether a tea is steamed, roasted, or ground into powder so you can pick the right preparation method.
2. **Keep lots distinct.** Treat each arrival as a separate lot (a defined batch of tea handled together) — this makes tasting and inventory review much easier.
3. **Share useful preparation details.** Give drinkers a water-to-tea ratio, water temperature, and steeping time as a dependable starting point.
