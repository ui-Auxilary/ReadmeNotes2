Review the fictional teaCapital returns and refund policy before requesting a return or exchange.

<Callout icon="📘" theme="info">
  **Fictional brand:** This page is sample documentation. teaCapital, its policies, and the examples below are fictional.
</Callout>

## What you can return

teaCapital accepts returns for orders that meet all of the following conditions:

- The return request is submitted within **30 days** of the delivery date.
- The tea is in its **original, unopened packaging**.
- The order was placed directly through teaCapital (not through a third-party retailer).

Opened tea cannot be returned because flavor and freshness degrade once the seal is broken.

## How to request a return

1. Locate your order number in your order confirmation email or account dashboard.
2. Contact teaCapital support with your order number and the reason for the return.
3. Wait for a return authorization and shipping instructions.
4. Pack the unopened tea securely and ship it using the provided instructions.

<Callout icon="💡" theme="info">
  **Keep your tracking number:** Hold on to the return shipment tracking number until the refund is confirmed.
</Callout>

## Refund timeline

Once teaCapital receives and inspects the returned tea, expect the following:

| Step | Estimated time |
| --- | --- |
| Inspection after delivery | 3–5 business days |
| Refund issued to original payment method | 5–10 business days after inspection |

Refunds are applied to the original payment method. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to a teaCapital error.

## Damaged or incorrect orders

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong tea, contact teaCapital support within **7 days** of delivery. Include a photo of the damage or the incorrect item. teaCapital will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost.

## Exchanges

To exchange an unopened tea for a different style, follow the standard return process and place a new order for the tea you want. teaCapital does not currently offer direct exchanges.

## Questions

For questions about a return or refund, contact teaCapital support with your order number. You can also review the [tea collection](/docs/tea-collection) to compare styles before reordering.
