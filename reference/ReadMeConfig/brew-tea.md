Scales a tea's default brewing recipe to the cup size you choose. The water temperature stays the same. The amount of tea changes in proportion to the water and is rounded to one decimal place.

Leave out `cup_ml` to get the default 200 ml recipe. Send your API key in the `X-TeaCapital-Key` header (see [Authentication](/reference/authentication)).
